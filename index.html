Features

Seven tools, one app

Everything opens from the home screen or the bottom bar, and the tools link to each other.

Periodic table

All 118 elements colored by category or by state at room temperature. Tap an element for its atomic mass, common ion and atomic structure.

Fe · 26 · 55.85 · [Ar] 3d6 4s2
  • Protons, neutrons and electrons for every element
  • Electron shells and configuration, including exceptions like Cr and Cu

Ions and formulas

Pick a cation and an anion to build the compound. The app balances the charges and names it.

Al3+ + SO42− → Al2(SO4)3
  • 16 cations and 14 anions, including polyatomic ions
  • Send the formula straight to the calculator

Reactions

42 common reactions with balanced coefficients, grouped into combustion, neutralization, synthesis, decomposition and replacement.

CH4 + 2O2 → CO2 + 2H2O
  • Atom check for each element and molar mass of every substance
  • Try balancing it yourself with − and + buttons

Calculator

Molar mass, moles, molarity and stoichiometry, each with the full solution shown step by step.

4 g H2 → 35.74 g H2O
  • Reads brackets and hydrates such as CuSO₄·5H₂O
  • Recent formulas and a list of common compounds

Molecules

30 molecules as rotatable 3D models: ball and stick, space-filling, or a Lewis structure.

NH3 · trigonal pyramidal · ≈ 107° · AX₃E
  • Shape, polarity, bond angle and VSEPR notation
  • Why each shape forms, from linear to square planar

Data tables

Ion table, solubility rules, metal reactivity series, indicators and pH, and physical constants.

NA = 6.02214076 × 1023 mol−1
  • Precipitate colors and indicator color-change ranges
  • Exact constants next to the rounded values you usually use

Search

Find elements, compounds, molecules, reactions and ions by name, symbol or formula from the home screen.

“na” → Na · NaCl · NaOH · 2Na + Cl2 → 2NaCl
  • Type a formula to calculate it right away
